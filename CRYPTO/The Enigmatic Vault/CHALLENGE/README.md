


The Enigmatic Vault




DIFFICULTY: Medium


SCENARIO:
You've intercepted an encrypted message from a mysterious organization.
Intelligence suggests they use multiple layers of encryption to protect
their secrets. Your mission: decrypt the message and find the flag.

FILES PROVIDED:
- message.enc
- hint.txt
- public_key.pem
- strange_data.bin

OBJECTIVE:
Find the flag in the format: NSC{...}

HINTS:
1. Not everything is as it seems
2. The layers run deep
3. Sometimes you need to look at the bigger picture
4. XOR is your friend, but which key?

Good luck, hacker!
