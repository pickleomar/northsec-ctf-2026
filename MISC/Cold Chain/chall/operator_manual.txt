Cold Chain Diagnostic Console
Warehouse Operations Extract

This document is the public operator handoff for the simulated refrigerated
warehouse. It intentionally describes the diagnostic interface, not the lost
routing table.

Units
-----

All times are integer controller ticks. Travel latency, switch response,
cooldown, scanner cycle, release times, and crate deadlines are reported in
ticks. Thermal drift and cooling load are integer accounting units used by the
simulator.

Briefing Fields
---------------

NODE <name> kind=<kind> cap=<n> heat=<n> cool=<n> tag=<hex>
  cap is the maximum number of crates that may occupy the node at once.
  heat is the per-tick thermal drift while waiting at the node.
  cool is the per-tick cooling load while waiting at the node.
  tag is the barcode alias emitted by nearby diagnostic receivers.

JUNCTION <name> states=<state,...> initial=<state>
  Junctions require switch grants before gated downstream moves. Switch commands
  have response latency and cooldown. A response sample may jitter by one tick;
  repeated valid samples are intended to be combined.

SCANNER cycle=<n> window=<n>
  Scanner gates accept reads when the corrected scanner tick falls inside the
  window beginning at zero. Desynchronized schedules are rejected.

Calibration Commands
--------------------

PULSE <node>
  Injects a low-energy tracer at a node. Downstream receivers may report
  delayed hits. Valid RX rows include sensor id, destination tag, travel delta,
  required switch state or OPEN, scanner lag, energy, heat, cooling, quality,
  and a checksum-like field. Some rows are marked ok=0 and should not be used.

ROUTE <src> <dst>
  Requests a controller route probe. The result is intentionally summarized:
  ETA range, a few sensors, and checksum-like fields. It is useful as a sanity
  check, not as a replacement for graph reconstruction.

SCAN <sensor>
  Reads a scanner clock diagnostic. Valid frames include an epoch reading and
  sensor skew. Dropped frames are marked ok=0. The scanner epoch is stable for
  the whole session.

TEMP <crate>
  Returns crate entry, exit, release tick, deadline, thermal budget,
  sensitivity, and barcode fragments. Barcode fragments include parity fields
  because real warehouse labels are not always read in one piece.

SWITCH <junction> <state>
  Runs a dry switch diagnostic. Valid ACK rows include response and cooldown
  samples. Response and cooldown samples may jitter by one tick.

Schedule Syntax
---------------

BEGIN
SYNC <phase>
<time> CRATE <id> ENTER <entry>
<time> SWITCH <junction> <state>
<time> CRATE <id> MOVE <downstream_node>
<time> CRATE <id> EXIT <exit>
END

The verifier sorts events by time. A MOVE line names the next node, not the
current node. The crate arrives after the calibrated travel latency.

Known Quirks
------------

Scanner receivers occasionally emit a dropped packet with plausible-looking
fields and ok=0. Do not average dropped rows.

PULSE travel deltas are deliberately noisy by one tick. The actual conveyor
latency is recoverable from the valid repeated samples.

Switch response and cooldown diagnostics are dry-run samples. They do not alter
the submitted schedule; only SWITCH lines inside the final schedule do.

Safety Constraints
------------------

The verifier rejects:

COLLISION AT T=...
SWITCH COOLDOWN VIOLATION
SWITCH STATE VIOLATION
CRATE EXPIRED
ENERGY LIMIT EXCEEDED
COOLING LIMIT EXCEEDED
SENSOR DESYNC
SCHEDULE FORMAT ERROR

There is no real-time race. The schedule is checked after submission.
